10 Service Codes
十项服务准则
Not just a hotel — a place where the heart comes home
Tap any code to expand · V3 · March 2026
01
Presence
在场感
Be fully here. Your attention is the service.
▾
Guest Service
Put down whatever you're doing when engaging with a guest. Maintain eye contact, don't interrupt, and respond to needs immediately.
Hotel Operations
Management walkthroughs are never just box-ticking. Stay focused — observe team state, spot and resolve guest needs proactively.
Back of House
Maintenance and housekeeping work with focus and efficiency. Don't disturb guests. Respond to urgent repair requests immediately.
02
Natural Ease
从容自然
Stay calm and unhurried. Let the experience unfold naturally.
▾
Guest Service
Keep a natural, unhurried manner. Avoid over-enthusiasm or scripted greetings — guests should feel sincerity, not performance.
Hotel Operations
The team's energy should feel calm and composed. Never let guests see stress or panic. Professionalism and ease go together.
Back of House
Back-of-house workflows should run smoothly. Reduce unnecessary urgency — a steady rhythm behind the scenes supports quality at the front.
03
Quiet Way
无声之道
Notice · Prepare · Act — before they ask.
▾
Guest Service · Stay Observant
Anticipate needs through observation — act before the guest has to ask.
Example: A guest's water glass is nearly empty. Refill it quietly without interrupting their conversation.
Hotel Operations · Data-Driven
Build observation systems that predict recurring guest needs.
Example: Data shows a high volume of family guests — pre-stock cribs on guest floors and proactively check in with families at arrival.
Back of House · Proactive Checks
Identify hazards and restock supplies without waiting to be asked.
Example: Housekeeping notices a loose door track — log it for repair on the spot rather than waiting for a complaint.
04
You Go First
先开口
Always be the first to smile. Always be the first to say hello.
▾
Guest Service
When you make eye contact with a guest, smile and greet them first — always. In corridors, the elevator, the restaurant — the team initiates.
Hotel Operations
Management proactively checks in with team members — ask about workload, challenges, and wellbeing. Build a culture where communication flows openly.
Back of House
Departments communicate proactively across teams — don't wait for others to raise issues. Reduce information gaps before they become problems.
05
Boundaries
边界感
Respect privacy, distance, and personal space.
▾
Guest Service
Don't pry into guests' personal matters. Maintain a comfortable social distance of about 1.5m. Never enter a guest room area without knocking first.
Example: Reception never shares information about a guest with a third party without the guest's consent. All in-room service requires knocking and confirmation first.
Hotel Operations
Sensitive guest information is strictly confidential — never disclosed to third parties. Private meetings, dining, and events have dedicated quiet spaces free from outside interference.
Example: When verifying guest information at the desk, the team member turns the screen away to protect privacy.
Back of House
Housekeeping and maintenance staff must never touch or move guests' personal belongings. Work quietly and stay focused — never discuss guest matters with colleagues during shifts.
06
Ownership
担当
See it. Take it. Fix it.
▾
Core Principle
When something goes wrong — don't deflect, don't wait, don't walk past it. Whether or not it's your job description, take it and drive it to resolution.
Guest Service
When a guest raises a complaint, take ownership immediately. No excuses, no passing the buck. Stay with it until it's resolved and confirmed.
Example: AC malfunction — the team member apologises at once and follows through on the repair, keeping the guest updated until it's fixed.
Hotel Operations
Management owns operational problems — accountability flows upward, not downward. When mistakes happen, acknowledge them, make it right, and debrief with the team.
Example: A double-booking error — apologise, upgrade the room, and hold a full team debrief afterwards.
Back of House
Shortages and equipment failures get resolved fast — no waiting around for someone to assign blame. Fix it first, review process after.
Example: Not enough crockery during service — source and redistribute immediately, then review inventory management.
07
Leave It Better
洁净如初
Every space, every time — cleaner than you found it.
▾
Guest Service · Respond Immediately
Clear and reset any guest-facing space promptly after use. After providing anything, ensure the setting is clean and presentable.
Example: Once a guest leaves their seat in the lobby, reset the sofa and replace the tableware immediately — return it to its best state.
Hotel Operations · Hold the Standard
Establish a clear cleaning responsibility system with defined standards and frequencies. Conduct regular spot checks.
Example: Maintain a Public Area Cleaning Manual with random audits to ensure consistent execution.
Back of House · Deep Clean
Use eco-friendly products and pay close attention to hidden areas — guest rooms, kitchen corners, overlooked surfaces.
Example: Departure cleaning must cover every corner thoroughly — the room should be ready to check in without a second pass.
08
Nature
顺应自然
Adjust to light, weather, season, and surroundings.
▾
Guest Service
Weave Moganshan's natural rhythms into every guest experience — seasonal dishes, bamboo forest walks, stillness in the morning mist. Let nature be the centrepiece, not the backdrop.
Hotel Operations
Reduce artificial intervention. Menus change with the seasons. Activities follow the weather and environment. Don't manufacture experiences — let them emerge naturally.
Back of House
Prioritise eco-friendly materials and sustainable sourcing. Minimise impact on the surrounding natural environment — care for the farm and bamboo forest ecosystem.
09
Safety Mindset
安全意识
Stay calm, stay alert — protect guests and each other.
▾
Guest Service
Always be aware of guests' physical safety — give extra attention to elderly guests, children, and guests with special needs. Any hazard spotted must be addressed or escalated immediately.
Hotel Operations
Regular safety training and fire drills ensure every team member knows emergency procedures. Safety equipment must be maintained and functional — never compromised for aesthetics.
Back of House
Engineering and maintenance must follow safety protocols strictly. Hazardous areas are marked and cordoned off immediately. Food storage must meet hygiene and safety standards at all times.
10
Respect Always
以礼待人
Everyone who walks through our door deserves to be treated with dignity.
▾
Guest Service
Treat every guest equally — regardless of appearance, age, or spend. Politeness and warmth are not conditional.
Example: Welcome an elderly guest in modest clothing with the same warmth and patience as anyone else — take time to explain room options fully, without rushing.
Hotel Operations
Management respects all team members. The team supports each other. Zero tolerance for workplace discrimination or disrespect.
Example: When a new team member makes a mistake, guide and encourage — don't humiliate. Use respectful language in all team communication.
Back of House
Respect applies across every role and partner. The same standards hold whether you're dealing with a supplier, a contractor, or a colleague in another department.
Example: Procurement negotiates with suppliers courteously. Housekeeping and service staff greet and give way to each other when passing in corridors.
